Responsibility split

GIC IAM
   │
   └── WHO are you?
       Which tenant are you associated with?
       Establish authentication / identity / tenant context.

GOS CONTROL PLANE
   │
   └── WHAT has this tenant purchased?
       Which modules, services, features and limits are enabled?

TENANT VMS
   │
   └── WHAT can this person/vendor do inside this tenant?
       Users, vendors, roles, permissions, policies and workflows.

GOS TELEMETRY
   │
   └── WHAT is actually happening?
       API calls, service usage, traces, logs, metrics, errors,
       performance and business usage.


Access rule:

          GOS ENTITLEMENT
                 ∩
          GIC IAM IDENTITY
                 ∩
          VMS PERMISSION
                 ∩
          RESOURCE POLICY
                 │
                 ▼
          EFFECTIVE ACCESS
